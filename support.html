Lectern Notes Support

For Lectern Notes on iPad · version 1.0 and later

Lectern Notes records your lectures, transcribes them on your iPad, and helps you turn the transcript into notes, a summary, quiz questions, flashcards and worked problems.

  • Need help? Email maxmcgee19@gmail.com. Please include your iPad model, your iPadOS version and what happened.
  • Diagnostics: Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Send diagnostics makes a small report you can attach to your email. It shows you what it contains first, and it holds no audio and no transcript text.

Requirements

  • An iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.
  • About 1.5 GB of free storage for the first download (the speech model itself takes about 490 MB), plus room for your recordings. The app warns you if storage runs low while you record.
  • An internet connection the first time you open the app, to download the speech model. After that, recording and transcription work offline.

Getting started

  1. Open the app. A welcome page explains the speech model and waits for you to tap Download (about 490 MB, from Hugging Face). It then prepares the model for your iPad and closes by itself. This takes a few minutes on good Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap Record at the start of a lecture and Stop at the end. The first time, iPadOS asks for permission to use the microphone.
  3. After Stop, open the lecture to read its transcript and make a study guide.

Common questions

Can I lock the screen while recording?

Yes. Recording continues when the screen is locked or you switch to another app. iPadOS does not let the app transcribe while it is not on screen, so the recorder shows how much is waiting, and the app transcribes those parts from the saved audio when you come back to it and after you press Stop. Keeping Lectern on screen lets it catch up sooner. A study guide waits until the transcript is complete.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once the speech model has downloaded. Recording and transcription happen on your iPad.

How do I make a study guide with Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?

Choose the service from the Study Guide menu. The first time, the app shows what will be shared and asks your permission. It then copies a prompt to your clipboard and opens the service; paste the prompt there, copy the reply, come back and tap Paste. If a reply cannot be imported, the app offers a fix-up prompt to paste back into the same chat. You need your own account with that service. You can withdraw permission in Settings ▸ About ▸ Privacy.

What about Apple Intelligence?

On iPads where Apple Intelligence is available and turned on, the app can write a first draft of notes on your iPad. These drafts are labelled as a first draft and can contain mistakes, especially in maths.

The app cannot hear me

Check that the microphone is allowed: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone, and turn on Lectern.

Where are my recordings kept?

On your iPad, inside the app. They are included in your iPad’s backups according to your backup settings. Deleting a lecture deletes its recording, transcript and study material; deleting the app deletes everything it stored. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Am I allowed to record my lectures?

Many universities and colleges have rules about recording classes. Please check your institution’s policy and get permission where it is required.
